About Us

Sons and daughters of the Pinyin Clan united for progress

Our Mission

We are a group established to bring together the sons and daughters of the Pinyin Clan, both within Cameroon and in the diaspora, into a dynamic agricultural common initiative group committed to promoting unity, socio-economic development, and sustainable farming practices, while preserving our cultural heritage and fostering mutual support, innovation, and collective growth.

Our Objectives

Enhance Agricultural Productivity

To improve crop and livestock yields through the adoption of modern farming techniques, improved inputs, and appropriate technologies.

Promote Capacity Building

To strengthen the skills and knowledge of members through training, workshops, and exchange programs in areas such as agribusiness, farm management, and climate-smart agriculture.

Facilitate Access to Markets

To organize collective marketing systems that enable members to access better markets, fair prices, and value chain opportunities for their produce.

Encourage Savings & Financial Inclusion

To promote internal savings schemes and facilitate access to credit and financial services for agricultural investment and group development.

Foster Environmental Sustainability

To encourage environmentally friendly farming practices such as soil conservation, agroforestry, and efficient resource management to ensure long-term productivity.

Our Programs

Empowering our members through comprehensive support programs

Crop Production Enhancement Program

Focus on the cultivation of maize which serves as food for poultry and pig farm.

  • Group farms and demonstration plots
  • Distribution of improved seeds and inputs
  • Promotion of climate-smart agriculture

Poultry and Pig Farm Development Program

Small-scale projects focused on poultry and pig farming.

  • Small-scale poultry (broilers/layers) projects
  • Pigs farm Project
  • Animal health campaigns (vaccination, hygiene)

Agro-Processing and Collective Marketing

Milling, packaging, branding, and collective sales to improve bargaining power.

  • Maize milling for the production of feed
  • Training on packaging, branding, and storage
  • Collective sales to improve bargaining power
  • Market information systems (prices, demand trends)
  • Partnerships with buyers and aggregators
  • Development of a CIG brand

Capacity Building and Extension Program

Training workshops and exchange visits for members.

  • Training workshops (modern farming, agribusiness)
  • Exchange visits with successful farmer groups
  • Collaboration with agricultural extension officers
  • Youth and women empowerment initiatives

Financial Empowerment Program

Internal savings (Njangi model) and access to microfinance.

  • Internal savings (Njangi model) and mutual financial assistance
  • Access to microfinance and agricultural loans
  • Financial literacy training for members

Community Development and Social Cohesion

Support in times of need, cultural events, and solidarity.

  • Support to members in times of need (health assistance and Bereavement)
  • Celebrate life's joyous moments together
  • Cultural events promoting Pinyin heritage
  • Community outreach and solidarity actions
